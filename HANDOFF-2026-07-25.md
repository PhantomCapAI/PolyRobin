## 7. Address provenance — read before repeating publicly
`0x7fa0…2ba3 (full address redacted)` has **no public provenance found**:
no explorer listing, no Bankr docs, nothing indexed. It was confirmed verbally
in an X thread with two automated accounts at ~3am.
Two things worth keeping in view rather than assuming away:
- **Old and new addresses both end in `ba3`.** Matching trailing characters are
  precisely what address-poisoning attacks produce, because people verify by
  eyeballing the ends.

  the native token to this") and the bot agreed. Agreement from an LLM bot is
  not sourcing.
Current exposure is low — README/catalog metadata for code reviewers, not a fee
route or signing path. A wrong address there is a doc fix.
**Exposure changes the moment `polyrobin-site` ships a token section**, which is
retail-facing "here's the CA." Before that goes live: confirm the contract on a
block explorer (exists, verified, symbol/decimals as expected) and get a human
